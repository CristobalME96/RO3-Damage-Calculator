RO3 damage calculator

Stages and term names come from the game client’s protocol file. The arithmetic comes from the ro3emu server, which is itself inferred, so treat results as estimates and check them against real hits in the calibration box.

Every hit is assumed to land (HIT and FLEE are ignored), and the crit figure assumes a crit happens. Magic hits don’t normally crit in the emulator, so for spells read it as a reference only. Not modelled: job and PvP parameters, the damage cap, buff and talent adjusters, and tower-pet rules.
